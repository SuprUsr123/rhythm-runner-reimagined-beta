Rhythm Runner ReimaginedLEVEL BUILDER

Desktop: Space jumps or flies. In Platformer Mode, use A/D or the arrow keys to move, and Space to jump. Touch users can use the on-screen direction buttons. Noclip ignores hazards and deadly sides. Hitboxes outline the player (thick box is the outer hitbox, small box is the inner one), solids (solid line) and hazards (dashed line).

Official levels are the 21 RobTop levels from the Lollipop mod. Each one is decoded when you pick it. Built-in levels come first.

Import accepts GD .gmd files (open them in a text editor and paste, or choose the file above), compressed level strings, and the level code shown by EXPORT CODE. Unsupported GD objects are skipped and listed.
